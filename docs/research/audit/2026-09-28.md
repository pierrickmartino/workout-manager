### 1. Close credential exposure response

**User problem:** previously exposed authentication material may retain account access or remain reachable through another ref. **Evidence:** the [incident checklist](../../security/incidents/2026-09-25-codex-tool-state.md) records remaining provider revocation, branch-tip and GitHub-native protection checks; current [secret-scanning CI](https://github.com/pierrickmartino/workout-manager/actions/runs/36333391370) passed. **Status:** **partially shipped** containment; credential validity and closure **unverified**. **Solution:** use provider-side account controls to revoke and confirm the opaque refresh credential, inspect/remediate retained refs, enable native secret scanning/push protection and verify with GitHub's dummy secret. Decide on history cleanup only after that evidence. **Benefit:** closes the largest trust risk rather than merely removing the current file. **Effort:** S/M. **Confidence:** high on missing closure evidence, unknown current credential validity. **Success:** all four boxes in the incident report are checked with dated, value-free evidence. The incident record is the existing work item; **no duplicate issue is drafted**.

### 2. Repair narrow-screen reflow

**User problem:** a 320px user may need horizontal scrolling to reach History controls or to finish naming exercises during creation. **Evidence:** the [split-fixture matrix](../../development/ui-layout-revalidation.md) measures History at 338–340px in all 48 portrait cells even with short names, and Creation at up to 789px for a spaced exercise name and 1,848px for unbroken input. It uses production fonts in isolated Chromium/WebKit components; it is not an authenticated iPhone test. [#562](https://github.com/pierrickmartino/workout-manager/issues/562) attributed the defect but did not fix the UI. **Status:** **partially shipped** responsive layout; reproduced isolated failures. **Solution:** allow History header actions to wrap/stack; constrain and wrap exercise-name columns/controls in Creation without truncating the editable meaning; retain keyboard focus and 200% text usability. **Benefit:** core controls stay reachable on compact phones. **Effort:** M. **Confidence:** high in fixtures, medium for production prevalence. **Success:** zero document overflow at 320px across the existing 48-cell Skin/mode/engine matrix with short, spaced and unbroken fixtures, plus one authenticated 200% text check. **New issue draft:** “Fix 320px History and Creation reflow”; acceptance criteria are those matrix and real-app checks.

### 3. Expose all plotted progress values

**User problem:** keyboard and screen-reader users cannot retrieve every Volume, Weekly Distance and Top-Set Trend point from pointer-only tooltips. **Evidence:** [CH-F1 and CH-F4](../../development/chart-accessibility-validation.md) record isolated focus and value-access checks against the [Volume chart](https://github.com/pierrickmartino/workout-manager/blob/8171439747c699029db3879fdf7c6ab47fec7fa6/apps/web/components/pulse/volume-chart.tsx#L26-L75), [Distance chart](https://github.com/pierrickmartino/workout-manager/blob/8171439747c699029db3879fdf7c6ab47fec7fa6/apps/web/components/pulse/distance-chart.tsx#L24-L55), and canonical Top-Set chart. Chart miniatures link to that canonical view but cannot solve missing values there. **Status:** **partially shipped** visuals; complete equivalent values **unverified/absent in inspected components**. **Solution:** add a compact, collapsible data list/table beside each chart using the exact logged-data projection, with dates (including year), value and unit; keep strength qualification rules intact. Check Muscle Balance year labels and atlas heat equivalence in the same accessibility pass. **Benefit:** everyone can inspect the same progress without inventing extra estimates. **Effort:** M. **Confidence:** high for isolated point-access gap, medium for screen-reader behavior. **Success:** every plotted point can be reached and read without a pointer, and VoiceOver/Safari plus NVDA/Firefox checks match a known logged fixture. **New issue draft:** “Provide complete accessible chart values”; acceptance criteria are point parity, dates/units, keyboard retrieval and the two spoken checks.

### 4. Correct Previous-attempt note contrast

**User problem:** a previous-performance hint becomes hard to read on some completed-set cards, weakening useful between-set context. **Evidence:** [rendered matrix](../../development/ui-layout-revalidation.md) measures 3.88–7.68:1, below 4.5:1 in 28 of 48 captures. Current [Previous hint](https://github.com/pierrickmartino/workout-manager/blob/8171439747c699029db3879fdf7c6ab47fec7fa6/apps/web/components/live-session-sets.tsx#L220-L231) uses a translucent Accent; [ADR-0081](../../adr/0081-all-text-bearing-skin-tokens-clear-the-contrast-floor.md) validates declared token pairings, not every nested composited state. [#560](https://github.com/pierrickmartino/workout-manager/issues/560) fixed the separate prescription note. **Status:** **partially shipped** contrast floor with one measured gap. **Solution:** use a resolved, full-opacity text token or adjust the completed-card pairing, then extend a rendered-state guard to this exact combination. **Benefit:** past-attempt context stays legible in every Skin and mode. **Effort:** S. **Confidence:** high in fixture. **Success:** all 48 Previous-hint captures reach at least 4.5:1 without regressing prescription-note ratios. **New issue draft:** “Meet contrast floor for Previous-attempt hint”; acceptance criterion is that rendered 48-cell check.

### 5. Respect reduced motion while loading

**User problem:** a motion-sensitive user still sees a spinning icon, sweep and pulsing skeleton after requesting reduced motion. **Evidence:** [34-check isolated resilience matrix](../../development/ui-resilience-validation.md) records four reduced-motion failures in Chromium/WebKit; current [GenerationProgress](https://github.com/pierrickmartino/workout-manager/blob/8171439747c699029db3879fdf7c6ab47fec7fa6/apps/web/components/GenerationProgress.tsx#L10-L36) and [Skeleton](https://github.com/pierrickmartino/workout-manager/blob/8171439747c699029db3879fdf7c6ab47fec7fa6/apps/web/components/pulse/skeleton.tsx#L10-L24) retain unconditional animations. Drawers passed reduced-motion checks. **Status:** **partially shipped** preference handling. **Solution:** disable those three animations under prefers-reduced-motion while retaining readable loading text and the stable placeholder size. **Benefit:** generation/loading remains understandable without unnecessary movement. **Effort:** S. **Confidence:** high in isolated browsers. **Success:** the existing initial and live-preference-change checks report no spinning/sweep/pulse in both engines and the text remains present. **New issue draft:** “Honor reduced motion in loading states”; acceptance criteria are those checks.

**Reduced-motion remediation update — 28 September 2026:** Shipped, with a scope wider than
this finding named. The three cited animations now stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`; so do
two the finding did not cover — `SyncStatusBanner`'s sync icon, and `catalog-detail`'s
hand-rolled placeholders, which were replaced by the shared `Skeleton` primitive so the
duplicate could not drift again. The generation sweep does not merely freeze: a stopped
third-width segment would rest at the left of its track and read as a determinate “33%
complete”, so under the preference the fill spans the track at reduced strength instead, and
the track's inert `aria-label` (on a `div` with no role, therefore ignored by assistive
technology) was replaced with `aria-hidden`, the `role="status"` region above it already
carrying the announcement. Eight `transition-transform` declarations were paired as well;
[ADR-0082](../../adr/0082-movement-does-not-survive-the-reduced-motion-preference.md) records
why the 48 `transition-colors` and one `transition-opacity` declarations are exempt by rule
rather than left undone. A fail-closed guard
([`motion-policy.ts`](../../../apps/web/lib/motion-policy.ts)) now sweeps every component in
the existing `web` CI job — it reproduced all 14 unguarded declarations before the fix and
reports none after. The `motion` journey fixture gained a syncing `SyncStatusBanner`, so the
banner has rendered rather than source-only evidence. **Evidence boundary:** both existing
reduced-motion checks pass in **Chromium only** — WebKit is not installed in the remediating
container, so the two-engine criterion in this finding is half-met, and transitions have no
browser evidence at all by design. See the dated remediation section of the
[resilience report](../../development/ui-resilience-validation.md).

**Previous-attempt contrast remediation — 28 September 2026:** Shipped, with a scope
wider than this finding named, and on different evidence than the success criterion
asked for. Both departures are set out here rather than left to be discovered.

*Scope.* The finding attributes the defect to completed-set cards. It is not theirs:
[`Card`](../../../apps/web/components/ui/card.tsx) is already `bg-surface` and the
completed branch re-declared it, so completed and in-progress cards had identical
backgrounds and the hint failed identically on both — including the current un-logged
card, where between-set context is most useful and which the fixture never captured.
The redundant class is removed. A sweep for call-site fades then found three more sites
in the same class, none named by this audit: a performed slot button at 2.67:1, and two
disabled pagers at 1.68:1. All four are fixed by deleting the fade — no new tokens and
no retuning. The two pagers had been provisionally treated as WCAG 1.4.3 inactive
controls; both render as a `<span>`, which is not a user interface component, so they
were fixed rather than exempted, and one gained the `aria-disabled` its twin already had.

*Evidence.* The 48-cell rendered check named as the acceptance criterion was **not**
re-run, and the reason is not that it could not be: after the fix the Previous hint is
`text-cyan` flat on `surface`, a pairing #562's rendered Accent sampler already measured
in situ at 5.47–6.01:1 for PULSE Light, within 1,728 samples whose minimum was 4.60006.
Re-capturing would re-measure a pairing that already has rendered evidence. What replaces
it is a static reproduction that matches this finding's rendered numbers exactly —
compositing cyan at 80% over each Skin's surface gives a 3.88:1 minimum and 7 of 12
failing variants, which is the reported 28 of 48 across two engines and two orientations —
plus a fail-closed CI guard
([`faded-text-policy.ts`](../../../apps/web/lib/faded-text-policy.ts)) that reproduced all
four violations before the fix and reports none after. The bar is the repository's
**4.6:1** `CONTRAST_FLOOR`, not the 4.5:1 this finding named.
[ADR-0083](../../adr/0083-a-call-site-fade-cannot-take-text-below-the-contrast-floor.md)
records the boundary: call-site fades are guarded, while element backgrounds and ancestor
`opacity-*` remain the browser harness's, with the two uncovered ancestor sites named by
line. Issues [#566](https://github.com/pierrickmartino/workout-manager/issues/566) and
[#567](https://github.com/pierrickmartino/workout-manager/issues/567) carry the fix and
the deferred undeclared-tint work.

## Boundaries

No repository application code was changed for this audit. No production usage, customer interviews, device run, actual screen-reader speech, authenticated browser journey, AI-generation quality sampling, or cost dashboard was available to this review. The cited browser reports are isolated, controlled fixtures; they are stronger than source inference but do not establish behavior on a signed-in phone. The proposed work respects plan/record separation, the self-paced calendar-free Protocol, typed Load and Quantity, and server read-time projections. A cached authenticated offline plan would conflict with [ADR-0028](../../adr/0028-web-pwa-is-installable-and-offline-aware-but-caches-no-authenticated-navigation.md) and would need an explicit owner decision before implementation.
